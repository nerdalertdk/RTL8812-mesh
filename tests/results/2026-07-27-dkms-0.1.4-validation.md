## Recovery behavior

After a later power cycle, distribution `rtl8xxxu` did not advertise mesh mode
for the experimental RTL8192FU. The hardened recovery helper returned 78 with
an explicit capability diagnosis before mutation; the complete `iw dev` hash
was identical before and after. Systemd is configured not to restart that
environmental failure.

Loading the existing exact-kernel mesh-enabled experimental `rtl8xxxu` artifact
restored peer capability. Udev recovery then reconstructed the open topology in
10 seconds and required bilateral traffic and one HWMP path at each peer before
returning success. This validates recovery orchestration but is not a
two-RTL8812AU production interoperability result.
