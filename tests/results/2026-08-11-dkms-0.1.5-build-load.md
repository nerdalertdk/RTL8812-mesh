## Production activation and mesh smoke gate

DKMS 0.1.5 was then installed under `updates/dkms`; the five loaded modules
matched the installed paths, `srcversion` values, hashes, and running-kernel
vermagic with no other `rtw_*` shared-core consumer.  The test configuration
was advanced atomically to `EXPECTED_VERSION=0.1.5` and the next module load
used `rtw_usb.switch_usb_mode=N`, the documented USB2 qualification policy.

The provenance-gated recovery service succeeded after the controlled load and
again when started explicitly:

- root `wlan1` and namespace peer `wlan2` both use `rtw_8812au`;
- both station tables reported `mesh plink: ESTAB`;
- each peer had one direct reciprocal HWMP path;
- five ICMP packets in each direction completed with zero loss.
