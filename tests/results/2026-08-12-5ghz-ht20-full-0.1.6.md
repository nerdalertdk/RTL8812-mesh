- Danish non-DFS channel 149 / 5745 MHz, HT20, enforced 13 dBm;

## Open mesh

- five serialized leave/down/up/rejoin cycles all passed, including bilateral
  peering, cold traffic, multicast reachability, and reciprocal HWMP paths;
- 64 MiB SHA-256-verified transfers passed at 5.38 MB/s root-to-peer and
  7.13 MB/s peer-to-root, with reciprocal postflight paths;

- each scoped interval had zero USB transport event.

## Secured mesh

The first SAE configuration used only `frequency=5745`; wpa_supplicant chose
VHT80, so that diagnostic success is deliberately excluded from HT20 evidence.
The corrected profile adds `disable_ht40=1` and `disable_vht=1`. While its
secured mesh was active, `iw dev info` independently verified both radios at
channel 149, width 20 MHz, 13 dBm (station reporting still labels its rate as
VHT MCS9, which is valid at 20 MHz).

The corrected run proved peer-specific SAE acceptance and decrypted AMPE,
MFP/authorization, bidirectional 20-packet unicast, multicast, reciprocal
HWMP, and protected 32 MiB SHA-256 transfers at 5.99 MB/s root-to-peer and
8.35 MB/s peer-to-root. It had zero transport event and provenance-safely
restored the normal open channel-1 HT20 mesh.
