# 5 GHz HT20 mesh smoke — 2026-08-12

## Scope

Two RTL8812AU adapters running exact DKMS 0.1.6 provenance joined the open
test mesh on non-DFS channel 149 (`5745 MHz`, HT20) under the active Danish
regulatory domain. That channel was advertised at the enforced 13 dBm limit.
The smoke then ran one serialized leave/down/up/join cycle and restored the
established channel-1 2.4 GHz HT20 topology regardless of outcome.

## Result

The 5 GHz recovery established bilateral mesh and HWMP paths in 12 seconds.
The lifecycle cycle established peers in 130 ms and passed all measured
checks:

```

# usb-errors-since-start
```

The scoped kernel interval contained no mac80211 warning, USB `-71`/
`-EPROTO`, disconnect/reset, or rtw88 RX/TX transport diagnostic. Both radios
were subsequently verified back on channel 1, HT20.

## Limit
